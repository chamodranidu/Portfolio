About Me

I am a passionate Engineer, driven by innovation, problem solving, and continuous learning. With a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Science and Technology (Specialized in Mechatronics) from Uva Wellassa University of Sri Lanka (GPA: 3.47, Second Class Upper Division), I bring strong technical expertise in designing, and optimizing electronics, automation systems, and custom machine solutions. I am highly motivated by engineering roles that involve designing and improving manufacturing facilities and processes while ensuring built-in quality and operational safety.

Whether developing mechanical systems and CAD designs, designing custom PCB layouts, programming microcontrollers, or integrating PLC-based industrial automation, I am committed to applying creative and practical engineering solutions to real-world challenges. With a strong focus on mechanical design, product development, prototyping, and system integration, I aim to contribute to technological advancement and deliver reliable, manufacturable solutions that support organizational growth.
